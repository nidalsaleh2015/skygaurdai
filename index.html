    Home/About

    Pigeons off the balcony, without harm

    Pigeons are a recurring problem around cities where they gather around windows, balconies and parking areas. Their acidic excrement causes structural damage to cars and structures while making biological waste. They cause mess, noise, and may pose health risks through local parasite transmission (Sulieman et al., 2026). Existing market deterrents depend on basic motion sensors that trigger aimlessly for cats, other bird species, or wind movement, causing repeated false alarms and animal adaptation. This makes them less impactful and accurate when solving pigeon related problems. This is where SkyGuard AI shows its value, it uses an enhanced edge AI vision model on local hardware to classify pigeons before setting oW targeted, harmless distinct sounds. This helps maintain safe, clean, and sustainable neighborhoods while also providing a custom, budget friendly solution that protects property while ensuring non target animals remain completely unharmed.

    Our mission

    Three goals, one device

    No harm to pigeons

    No spikes, nets, glue or poison. Birds are never touched. A short sound pulse is enough to make a pigeon choose another place to rest.

    Safe property

    Pigeon droppings are acidic and stain balconies, furniture and AC units, and nests can block drains and vents. Keeping pigeons away protects homes and cuts cleaning and repair costs.

    Clean environment

    Fewer droppings means fewer bacteria and fungi near windows and living spaces, and less use of strong cleaning chemicals on balconies.

    The problem

    Most deterrents either hurt birds or affect everyone.

    Pigeons gather on apartment balconies across the UAE. Spikes and glue hurt birds. Nets block the view. Always-on sound devices affect every bird and every person nearby, and many "ultrasonic" repellers use frequencies pigeons barely hear, so they do little.

    Our idea

    Only act when it is really a pigeon.

    An AI model checks each bird the camera sees. When it is confident a bird is a pigeon, a speaker plays a short pulse in the range pigeons hear best (about 1–4 kHz), changing the sound each time so they do not get used to it. Doves, mynas, sparrows and parakeets are left alone.

    Home/Product

    The SkyGuard AI unit

    A small weather-proof box that mounts in the corner of a balcony. Inside are a camera, a low-power computer that runs the AI model, and a speaker. It works on its own, with no app needed.

    Inside the unit

    Prototype parts

    Computer
    Raspberry Pi, or ESP32-CAM for a low-cost version
    Camera
    Camera module with a wide-angle lens
    AI model
    Object detector (for example YOLO) trained on photos of local birds
    Deterrent
    Small amplified speaker, 1–4 kHz pulses, volume limited
    Power
    Mains adapter; solar panel as a future option
    Logging
    Detections and pulses saved to a memory card

    How it works

    From camera frame to flight in five steps

    1. WatchThe camera checks the balcony about 10 times a second.
    2. DetectThe AI finds every bird in the picture and draws a box around it.
    3. ClassifyIt decides the species and how sure it is, for example "pigeon, 0.92".
    4. DecideIt acts only for a confident pigeon inside the balcony zone for 0.6 s.
    5. DeterA short, varied pulse plays. The pigeon flies away unharmed, then the unit waits 3 s.
